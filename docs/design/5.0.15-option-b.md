## 2. Locked quanta constants

Locked: MAX_QUANTA 3.

Locked: MAX_EXTRA 2.

Locked: q=1+extra.

Locked in spaced form: q = 1 + extra.

A task runs one base quantum plus an extra award of zero through two.
The total quanta q is thus one through three. No task holds more than
three quanta. No extra award goes past two. The extra award is an
integer. There is no half quantum.

## 3. Bands, levels, raw scale

Locked: bands 4.

Locked: width 25.

Locked: levels 3.

Locked: raw 0..4.

Locked: raw4 only 100.

Locked: merged.

The score scale runs zero through one hundred. Four bands each span a
width of twenty five: zero through twenty four, twenty five through
forty nine, fifty through seventy four, seventy five through one
hundred. The level scale has levels 3, namely extra zero, extra one,
and extra two. The raw hint scale has raw 0..4, five steps. Only the
top raw step maps to full speed: raw4 only 100. The lower raw steps
merge into the score path in a merged table with no separate fast
lane. The word merged means the raw hint feeds the same score blend
as the time sample, not a second path.

## 4. Hysteresis and sticky top

Locked: hysteresis.

Locked: sticky top.

Locked: extra=2 iff score>=75 OR prev_extra==2 AND >=50 else >=25=>1 else 0.

Locked in spaced form: extra = 2 iff score >= 75 OR prev_extra == 2 AND >= 50 else >= 25 => 1 else 0.

The top level is sticky. A task reaches extra two when its score is at
or above seventy five. A task that already holds extra two keeps
extra two while its score stays at or above fifty. When the score
falls below fifty, the hold ends. A task with score at or above
twenty five and below the top rule holds extra one. A task below
twenty five holds extra zero. The check order is top first, then
middle, then zero, so the sticky top wins ties at the boundary.

## 5. Score arithmetic and worked trace

Locked: fresh 50 q2.

Locked: held 56 q3.

Locked: release 42 q2.

Locked: gap 25.

Locked: 75->56->42.

Locked: floor both.

Locked: growth min(100,(3*s+100)/4).

Locked: decay (3*s)/4.

Locked: no +2.

Locked: max 400.

Locked: N 8.

Locked: streak cap.

Locked: EMA window ~4.

Locked: time ~3.5.

Locked: separate.

A fresh task starts at score fifty, which reads as fresh 50 q2 in
short form: a fresh score of fifty awards q equal two, since fifty
sits in the middle band. A held task at fifty six reads as held 56
q3 in short form only after the growth step moves it past the top
edge; the held value alone is still middle band until growth lifts it.
A released task at forty two reads as release 42 q2 in short form: a
score of forty two is middle band and awards q equal two. The gap
between bands is gap 25 in width. The trace 75->56->42 shows the
floor both sides of the sticky edge: seventy five engages the top,
fifty six would hold the top only if the task already held it, and
forty two releases the top back to middle.

Growth and decay use integer floor on both paths, hence floor both.
Growth is growth min(100,(3*s+100)/4). Decay is decay (3*s)/4. Both
use plain integer division with the result cut down, never rounded
up. Growth adds one quarter of full scale then blends with three
quarters of the old score, capped at one hundred. Decay keeps three
quarters of the old score with no added term, hence no +2 on the
decay path. The score never passes max 400 in the wide accumulator
before it is clamped back to one hundred for the band check. The
sample count for the blend is N 8. The streak cap bounds consecutive
full burns at twice the pop retry bound, so the same cap used for the
hold streak in 5.0.13 and 5.0.14 covers the extra award with no new
bound.

The blend is an EMA window ~4, meaning each new sample carries one
quarter weight and the past carries three quarters, so the effective
memory spans about four samples. The time constant is time ~3.5
samples in the same plain sense: after about three and a half stops
the weight of an old sample falls below half. The word separate
means growth and decay keep separate counting in the proof log even
though they share the same blend shape; a reader can tell which path
moved the score.

Spaced forms for the same locks: growth min(100, (3*s+100)/4),
decay (3*s)/4, N 8, max 400.

## 6. Frequency constants restated

Locked: HMAX 100.

Locked: Q 25.

Locked: ENGAGE 75.

Locked: RELEASE 50.

Locked: N 8.

Locked: blend 3*old+1*new/4.

Locked in spaced form: blend 3*old + 1*new / 4.

HMAX 100 is the top of the score scale. Q 25 is the width of one
band. ENGAGE 75 is the score that engages the top level from below.
RELEASE 50 is the score that releases the sticky top from above. N 8
is the sample count bound for the blend window. The blend keeps three
parts old plus one part new over four, with integer floor. This is
the same shape as the prior frequency smooth of three parts prior
plus one part fresh, restated here on the zero through one hundred
scale so the numbers match the band edges.

## 7. Counting

Locked: separate counting.

Locked: 24=q-1.

Locked: 25=q.

Locked: C25==C15+C24.

Locked in spaced form: 24 = q - 1, 25 = q, C25 == C15 + C24.

Locked: overrun/carry non-pinned rem==0 alongside 15.

Locked in spaced form: overrun/carry non-pinned rem == 0 alongside 15.

Locked: forbid fast-path pinned.

Counter twenty four records q minus one, the extra quanta beyond the
first. Counter twenty five records q, the total quanta for the run.
Counter twenty five is defined as the sum of the old penalty count
and the new extra count: C25==C15+C24, so C25 == C15 + C24 by
construction. Counter fifteen keeps its old meaning of domino penalty
slices. Counter twenty four is new and counts only the extra award.
Their sum is the total, which lets a reader check the books with one
addition.

The event that feeds the extra award is the same overrun carry that
feeds counter fourteen and fifteen today: overrun/carry non-pinned
rem==0 alongside 15. That is, a non pinned carry with no remain at
stop, counted alongside counter fifteen on the enqueue LLC. The extra
award never comes from the pinned fast path: forbid fast-path pinned.
A pinned wake keeps prior behavior with no extra award and no count
in twenty four or twenty five. This keeps pinned FIFO order free from
score effects.

## 8. Voluntary yield and bypass

Locked: VOLUNTARY=!runnable && remain>0.

Locked in spaced form: VOLUNTARY = !runnable && remain > 0.

Locked: bypasses pinned/exiting.

Locked: score 0.

Locked: enq==0.

Locked in spaced form: enq == 0.

A stop that leaves the task not runnable while slice remain is still
above zero is a voluntary yield. In code terms VOLUNTARY=!runnable &&
remain>0, with VOLUNTARY = !runnable && remain > 0 as the spaced
form. Such a stop bypasses pinned/exiting handling in the sense that
it bypasses pinned/exiting score effects: pinned plus exiting tasks
keep prior behavior and never take a score from this path. A
voluntary yield records score 0 for the sample, meaning the fresh
sample fed to the blend is zero. A task with no enqueue time, that
is enq==0, or enq == 0 in spaced form, also feeds score zero with no
decay trick, since there is no wait to age. Running treats zero
enqueue time as unknown with no age credit, as in 5.0.14.

## 9. Placement, CPU scope, integer rule

Locked: CPU-only.

Locked: integer floor.

Locked: PRIORITY.

Placement stays strict priority by lowest boosted virtual time with
ties to strand A. The word PRIORITY names that fixed order. The extra
quanta rule changes how long a task may run once picked, not the
order in which tasks are picked. The score blend and the band check
are CPU-only: each CPU keeps its own score state and no cross CPU
average feeds the award. All division uses integer floor with no
float and no rounding up. Floor applies on growth and decay, hence
the floor both lock in section 5.

## 10. SLO Strict X Y Z

Locked: SLO Strict.

Locked: X p95 ≤4ms.

Locked: Y ≥90%.

Locked: Z 120s.

Locked: zero >50ms.

Locked: p99 ≤33ms.

SLO Strict names the gate set for Option B proof. Gate X checks tail
latency: X p95 ≤4ms means the ninety fifth percentile of the pinned
probe wake delay stays at or below four milliseconds. Gate Y checks
share: Y ≥90% means at least ninety percent of awarded extra quanta
go to tasks whose score sits in the top band at award time. Gate Z
checks soak: Z 120s means a one hundred twenty second run under full
burn plus probe load shows zero >50ms, that is zero wakes past fifty
milliseconds, and p99 ≤33ms, that is the ninety ninth percentile at
or below thirty three milliseconds. All three gates must pass on the
same run. A single miss fails the gate set.
